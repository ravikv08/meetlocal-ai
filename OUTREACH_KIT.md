# Outreach Kit — Founding 50 + First Travelers

> Ready-to-paste messages for activating your first 30-50 users. Sequenced
> in waves so you learn from each batch before sending the next.

---

## The strategy in 2 lines

1. **Locals first, then travelers** — supply must exist before demand can find anything to book. Activate 10 Miami locals, then invite the first travelers.
2. **Three waves, not a blast** — Wave 1 (warmest 5) this week. Wave 2 (next 15) after you learn. Wave 3 (remaining 30) after Wave 2's bookings work.

---

### Wave 2 — Next 15 (week 2)

After Wave 1, you'll know:
- Which channel converted best
- Which value prop landed
- What concerns kept showing up

**Rewrite the message for Wave 2** based on what Wave 1 taught you. Don't reuse Wave 1's copy verbatim. The "best message" is the one your first 5 conversations gave you.

### Wave 3 — Final 30 (week 3-4)

Only run Wave 3 if Wave 2's locals are actually completing bookings. If they're not, hold the wave — you have a supply problem to solve before more applicants.

## ► The "Collison installation" — what happens after they say yes

Per [STRATEGY_NOTES.md § 13](STRATEGY_NOTES.md). Critical for first 30 users.

When someone replies *"yes, tell me more"*:

> **DO NOT say "great, sign up at meetlocal.ai!"**
>
> Say: *"Let's do 10 min on FaceTime / Zoom — I'll walk you through it and personally vouch for the local I introduce you to. What time works?"*

On the call:
1. **First 5 min:** customer discovery in disguise. Ask about their last solo trip / city plans / what they're hoping for.
2. **Next 2 min:** describe meetlocal + show them their match (you've pre-picked a local you think fits)
3. **Last 3 min:** walk them through the booking on screen-share. Your Stripe link, your platform, but YOU drove the experience.
4. **Send the 3-way intro text immediately:** *"[Local], meet [Traveler]. They're landing Friday. [Local] — your friend."*

This is non-scalable on purpose. You'll do this 30 times. By traveler 30 you'll know exactly what predicts a great match — and *that* is what your eventual algorithm gets trained on.

## ► Admin approval workflow — quick reference

When a local applies via your invite:

| Step | URL | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **/admin** | See new pending app at top with KYC badges |
| 2 | Click **Review →** | Opens /admin/locals/<id> |
| 3 | | Verify ID + selfie match. Check social links work. Read bio. |
| 4 | Toggle **ID verified** ✓ + **Background checked** ✓ | (For Founding 50, founder vouches in place of vendor BG check) |
| 5 | Click **Approve & activate** | Local goes live, appears in city searches |
| 6 | Text/DM them | "You're live! Your profile is at meetlocal.ai/local/<id>. Add to your IG bio if you want." |

**Time per approval: ~5 min once you have the rhythm.**

## ► First-day script for a traveler who lands their first match

After the 3-way intro, send this to the traveler within 1 hour of their meet starting:

```
Hey [name] — just checking in. How's it going with [local]?
Reply 👍 if all good, anything else if you want me to step in.

— Ravi (founder, meetlocal)
```

This is **personal safety presence + product UX research** simultaneously. Their reply tells you everything about how the meet is going. The Wave 1 travelers will get this. Wave 3 won't need to.

## ► When to graduate from this manual mode

Per [LAUNCH_STAGES.md § 1](LAUNCH_STAGES.md):

| Gate | Pass criteria |
|---|---|
| Stage 0 → 1 | **30 hand-touched bookings completed**, ≥40% Sean Ellis "very disappointed", zero safety incidents |
| Stage 1 → 2 | 200 bookings, no founder hand-matching needed for >80% of them |

You're in **Stage 0** today. You'll be in Stage 0 until ~bookings #30 — which is the entire 50-person network you have lined up. Don't try to graduate early.
