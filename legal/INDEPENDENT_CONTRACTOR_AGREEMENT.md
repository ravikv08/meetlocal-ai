## 1. Independent contractor status

### 1.1 Acknowledgment
The Contractor and the Company expressly intend that the Contractor is
an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, partner, joint
venturer, or franchisee of the Company. The Contractor:
- Sets their own hourly rate (subject to platform minimums)
- Determines their own availability and decides which booking requests
  to accept or decline
- Controls the methods, location, and timing of each meet
- Provides their own equipment, transportation, and tools
- Is free to perform similar services for any other person or company,
  including competitors of the Company
- Has no fixed work hours or set schedule
- Receives no W-2 wages, employee benefits, workers' compensation,
  unemployment insurance, or paid time off

### 1.2 What the Company does (and does not) do
The Company:
- Operates a technology platform that connects Locals with Travelers
- Sets baseline quality and safety standards (e.g., vetting, ToS)
- Sets a 30-minute response window for booking requests
- Charges a platform fee
- Provides access to payment processing (Stripe)

The Company does **not**:
- Direct *how* the Contractor conducts experiences
- Require minimum hours of availability
- Provide training (other than optional onboarding resources)
- Reimburse expenses
- Withhold income or payroll taxes

### 1.3 Tax obligations
The Contractor is solely responsible for:
- Reporting all income earned through the Platform
- Paying all federal, state, and local income taxes
- Paying self-employment tax (FICA equivalent)
- Obtaining any business licenses or permits required by their
  jurisdiction
- Filing Form 1040 Schedule C and any state equivalents

The Company will issue a Form **1099-NEC** to any Contractor earning
$600 or more in a calendar year, as required by IRS rules. Where the
Company uses Stripe Connect Express, Stripe may issue 1099 forms on the
Company's behalf.

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Description
The Contractor provides 1:1 in-person experiences ("**Experiences**") to
Travelers booked through the Platform.

### 2.2 Experience standards
The Contractor agrees that each Experience will:
- Match what the Contractor advertised on their LocalProfile
- Be conducted lawfully and safely
- Comply with the Company's [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md)

### 2.3 Right to refuse
The Contractor may decline any booking for any non-discriminatory
reason. The Contractor is not required to accept any minimum number of
bookings.

---

## 3. Compensation and payment

### 3.1 Rate
The Contractor sets their own hourly rate, subject to the Platform
minimum (currently **$15/hour**). The Contractor may adjust their rate
at any time.

### 3.2 Platform fee
The Company deducts a platform fee (currently **22% of subtotal**) from
the Contractor's payout. The Contractor receives the remaining
**78%** of the booking subtotal, paid through Stripe Connect or
equivalent.

### 3.3 Payout schedule
Payouts are made on a regular schedule (currently **weekly**) to the
Contractor's verified bank account. Stripe and the Contractor's bank
control the actual transfer time.

### 3.4 Chargebacks and refunds
If a Traveler initiates a chargeback or is granted a refund:
- For a chargeback won by the Traveler: the chargeback amount may be
  deducted from the Contractor's future payouts.
- For a Company-approved refund due to Contractor no-show or quality
  issue: the refunded amount is deducted from the Contractor's payout.
- For a Company-approved refund due to force majeure: no deduction.

### 3.5 No advance against future earnings
The Company does not advance funds against future earnings. The
Contractor is only paid for completed Experiences.

## 4. Compliance and verification

### 4.1 Identity verification
The Contractor authorizes the Company and its third-party providers to
verify the Contractor's identity, including reviewing a government-
issued ID and a live selfie.

### 4.2 Background check
By applying, the Contractor authorizes the Company and its third-party
providers (e.g., Persona, Checkr) to obtain a consumer report under the
Fair Credit Reporting Act ("**FCRA**"). This may include a criminal
background check, sex-offender registry check, and identity
verification.

The Contractor will receive a separate FCRA disclosure and consent form
before any background check is run.

### 4.3 Status changes
The Company may suspend, pause, or remove the Contractor at any time
for:
- Violations of this Agreement, the Terms of Service, or Acceptable Use
  Policy
- Failed or expired identity verification or background check
- Repeated cancellations, no-shows, or low review scores
- Safety reports

### 4.4 Right to deactivation appeal
If the Contractor's account is deactivated, they may request a review
within **30 days** by emailing **[support@meetlocal.ai]**.

## 5. Intellectual property

### 5.1 Contractor content
The Contractor retains ownership of their profile content (photos, bio,
reviews, messages). The Contractor grants the Company a worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable license to use, display, and
promote that content in connection with the Platform.

### 5.2 Company marks
The Contractor may not use the Company's name, logo, or trademarks
without prior written permission, except to refer to the Platform in
the ordinary course of accepting bookings.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

The Contractor agrees not to disclose any non-public Company information
(business plans, financials, customer lists, source code, vendor
agreements) except as required to perform Experiences. This obligation
survives termination of this Agreement for **3 years**.

---

## 7. Insurance

The Contractor is responsible for any insurance they wish to carry
(general liability, automotive, etc.). The Company does **not** provide
liability or accident insurance for Locals.

---

## 8. Indemnification

The Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Company and
its officers, directors, employees, and agents from any claims, damages,
liabilities, losses, and expenses arising from:
- The Contractor's conduct during Experiences
- The Contractor's violation of this Agreement
- The Contractor's violation of any law or third-party right

---

## 9. Termination

### 9.1 Either party may terminate
Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without
cause, by providing notice through the Platform.

### 9.2 Effect of termination
Upon termination:
- The Contractor's LocalProfile is set to `removed` status
- Pending bookings may be cancelled at the Company's discretion
- The Contractor receives any earned and pending payouts on the next
  scheduled payout cycle
- Sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, and 11 survive termination
