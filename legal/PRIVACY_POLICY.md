## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information you give us
- **Account info:** name, email, profile photo, password (via OAuth provider)
- **Local applicant info:** date of birth, government-issued ID, live selfie, social media handles, bio, hourly rate, languages, residency, neighborhood, photos
- **Payment info:** stored and processed by **Stripe** (we do not store full card numbers)
- **Communications:** in-Platform messages, support emails, reviews, notes on bookings
- **Verification info:** background-check responses (when applicable)

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** pages visited, bookings created, search queries, time on page
- **Device data:** IP address, browser, OS, device identifiers, language
- **Cookies & local storage:** see [Cookie Policy](COOKIE_POLICY.md)
- **Approximate location:** derived from IP (not GPS unless you grant it)

### 1.3 Information from third parties
- **Google OAuth:** email, name, profile picture (with your consent)
- **Identity verification providers** (e.g., Persona, Stripe Identity): KYC results
- **Background-check vendors** (e.g., Checkr): consumer reports under FCRA
- **Stripe:** payment, payout, and risk signals

We do not buy data brokers' data, and we do not sell your data to anyone
(see §6).

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- **Operate the Platform** — match Travelers and Locals, process bookings, route messages
- **Process payments** — authorize, capture, refund, and pay out via Stripe
- **Verify identity** — KYC and background checks for Locals
- **Detect fraud and abuse** — monitor for suspicious account behavior
- **Provide support** — answer your inquiries
- **Send transactional emails** — booking notifications, account events
- **Improve the product** — analytics, A/B testing, debugging
- **Comply with legal obligations** — taxes, court orders, regulatory requests

We do **not** use your information for behavioral advertising or to train
generative AI models.

## 3. Legal bases (GDPR users)

We process data under the following legal bases:
- **Contract performance** (most processing — providing the service)
- **Legal obligation** (taxes, court orders, anti-fraud)
- **Legitimate interest** (security, analytics, abuse prevention)
- **Consent** (cookies, optional marketing communications)

Where we rely on legitimate interest, you may object as described in §7.

## 4. Sharing of information

We share information with:
- **Locals/Travelers in your bookings** — first name, photo, public profile
- **Stripe** — payment + payout processing
- **Supabase** — database + storage hosting
- **Resend** — transactional email
- **Identity & background-check vendors** — only what's required
- **Google** — OAuth sign-in identity provider
- **Service providers under DPAs** — analytics, error tracking (e.g., Sentry)
- **Successors in interest** — if we are acquired or merge
- **Legal authorities** — when required by law or legal process
- **Anyone you ask us to share with** — with your explicit consent

We require all service providers to sign data-processing agreements
limiting their use of your data to the purposes we specify.

## 5. International transfers

We process data in the **United States**. If you access the Platform from
outside the US, your data is transferred to the US under **Standard
Contractual Clauses** (EU/UK GDPR) or other lawful mechanisms. By using
the Platform you consent to this transfer.

## 6. We do not sell your personal information

We do not sell your personal information for monetary or other valuable
consideration as defined under CCPA/CPRA or comparable laws. We do not
share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have rights to:

| Right | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you |
| **Correction** | Ask us to fix inaccurate data |
| **Deletion** | Ask us to delete your data (with limits — see §8) |

| **Restriction** | Limit how we use your data |
| **Objection** | Object to processing based on legitimate interest |

To exercise rights, email **[CONTACT EMAIL, e.g., privacy@meetlocal.ai]**

For California users, you may designate an authorized agent to act on

## 8. Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, plus:
- **Booking + payment records:** **7 years** (tax compliance)
- **KYC documents (ID, selfie, DOB):** **7 years** after last activity (fraud prevention + dispute defense)
- **Communications:** **3 years** after last booking
- **Messages on Realtime channels:** **2 years**
- **Reviews:** indefinitely (anonymized after account deletion)
- **Background-check reports:** **5 years** (FCRA standard)

When the retention period ends or you delete your account, we either
delete or anonymize your data. We may retain data longer if legally
required.

## 9. Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:
- TLS encryption for all data in transit
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data (KYC documents, message content)
- Secure password storage via OAuth (we don't store passwords)
- Role-based access controls
- Regular security reviews
- Vendor SOC 2 or equivalent compliance for processors

No method is 100% secure. We will notify affected users and authorities
of any data breach as required by applicable law (typically within 72
hours under GDPR).

## 10. Children's privacy

The Platform is not intended for users under **18 years old**. We do not
knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has
provided us data, contact **[CONTACT EMAIL]** and we will delete it.
