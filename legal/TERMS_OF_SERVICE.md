## 4. The booking flow

### 4.1 Traveler booking
A Traveler initiates a booking by submitting a request through the
Platform, including the date, time, duration, and any optional meeting
spot. Upon submission, the Traveler authorizes a payment hold (not a
charge) on their selected payment method via our payment processor,
Stripe.

### 4.2 Local accept window
The Local has a window (currently **fourteen (14) minutes**, subject to
change) to accept or decline the booking request. If the Local accepts,
the payment is captured and the booking is confirmed. If the Local
declines or fails to respond, the payment hold is released and the
booking is cancelled.

### 4.3 Manual capture & timing
We use Stripe's manual-capture feature: payment is **authorized at booking
time** and **captured only on Local accept**. If a payment hold expires
before capture (typically after seven days), the booking is automatically
cancelled.

### 4.4 Conduct of the experience
Once confirmed, the meet takes place at the agreed time and location.
Both parties may chat through the Platform's real-time messaging feature
in advance.

### 4.5 Completion & reviews
After the meet, either party may mark the booking complete. Both parties
are then prompted to leave a review. Reviews remain hidden until **both**
parties submit, after which they become visible simultaneously on the
Local's public profile.
