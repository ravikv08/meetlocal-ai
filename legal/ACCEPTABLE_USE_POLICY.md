This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") applies to all users of the
meetlocal.ai platform. It is incorporated into our [Terms of Service
](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) by reference. Violations may result in immediate
account suspension, removal, refund denial, and reporting to law
enforcement.

---

## You may NOT use the Platform to:

### 1. Harm others
- Harass, threaten, intimidate, stalk, or assault any person
- Engage in or facilitate human trafficking, prostitution, or any
  commercial sex acts
- Discriminate against any person on the basis of race, ethnicity,
  national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender
  identity, age, marital status, disability, or veteran status
- Engage in any activity that endangers the physical safety of another
  user

### 2. Engage in illegal conduct
- Sell, distribute, or use illegal drugs
- Possess, brandish, or use weapons
- Engage in fraud, theft, or money laundering
- Violate any local, state, federal, or international law during a meet

### 3. Circumvent the Platform
- Solicit Travelers or Locals to transact off-platform to avoid the
  platform fee
- Use the messaging system to share contact info before booking confirmation
- Operate multiple accounts to evade suspensions
- Manipulate reviews (e.g., self-reviews, threats to extract reviews)

### 4. Misrepresent yourself
- Use a false identity, photo, or bio
- Provide a forged or altered government ID for verification
- Misrepresent your qualifications, experience, or city of residence
- Fail to disclose facts material to the safety of a meet

### 5. Violate IP or other rights
- Upload content you don't own or have rights to
- Use the Platform to distribute pirated material
- Scrape, mirror, or republish Platform content without permission
- Reverse-engineer the Platform

### 6. Damage the Platform
- Upload malware, viruses, or any harmful code
- Conduct denial-of-service attacks
- Probe for security vulnerabilities without our written permission
- Bypass rate limits or any technical restrictions

### 7. Misuse payments
- Initiate fraudulent chargebacks
- Use stolen payment instruments
- Use the Platform for any payment unrelated to a legitimate Experience

### 8. Engage in conduct unbecoming of the Platform
- Public intoxication that endangers a Traveler
- Engage in undisclosed political or religious solicitation
- Make sexual advances or comments toward Travelers
- Bring others not booked to the meet
- Pressure a Traveler to extend the meet beyond what was booked

---

## What we will do if you violate this AUP

Depending on severity:
- **Warning** (first minor violation, with chance to cure)
- **Account suspension** (temporary, pending review)
- **Account removal** (permanent, with no refund of platform fees)
- **Refund withholding** (no refund will be issued)
- **Payout withholding** (any earned payouts may be withheld)
- **Report to law enforcement** (for crimes, threats, or safety incidents)
- **Legal action** (we may sue for damages)

We exercise these remedies at our sole discretion.

---

## How to report

If you witness or experience a violation, report it immediately:
- In the Platform: open the chat or profile and tap **Report**
- Email: **[safety@meetlocal.ai]**
- Emergency: contact local law enforcement first, then notify us

We respond to safety reports within **24 hours** and treat them
confidentially.
